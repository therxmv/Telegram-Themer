## Description of the App

Themer lets you create custom Telegram themes (Android `.attheme` and iOS `.tgios-theme` files) with live
preview, light/dark/AMOLED modes, gradients, and Monet color support. The App generates theme files for you
to export and apply within the official Telegram app. The App is free to use, contains no advertisements,
and offers no in-app purchases or subscriptions.
